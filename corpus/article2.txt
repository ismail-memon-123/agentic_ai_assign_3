The Republic of Aurelia is a fictional island nation located in the Azure Expanse approximately 600 kilometers east of the Meridian Coast. Although it appears on no real-world maps, Aurelia has been described in historical fiction and speculative geography as one of the oldest continuously inhabited island civilizations. The country consists of seven major islands and dozens of smaller volcanic islets, covering a total area of 48,300 square kilometers.

The capital city of Aurelia is Solhaven, located on the southeastern coast of the largest island, Veridian Isle. Solhaven is known for its white marble government buildings, extensive canal system, and the historic Observatory of Tides, which has operated continuously since 1124 A.E. (Aurelian Era). The city's population is approximately 1.9 million residents.

Aurelia has an estimated national population of 12.8 million people. The official language is Aurelian, while English is commonly spoken in business and tourism. The national currency is the Solaris Crown (SC). One Solaris Crown is divided into 100 Lumens.

The current President of Aurelia is Dr. Elian Marrow, who assumed office in March 2024 after winning the national election with 58 percent of the popular vote. The Prime Minister is Sophia Vey, who oversees the Council of Ministers responsible for economic development, education, transportation, and scientific research.

Aurelia operates under a parliamentary republic. The National Assembly contains 180 elected representatives serving four-year terms. Elections are conducted using proportional representation across twelve administrative provinces.

The country's economy is valued at approximately 412 billion Solaris Crowns annually. Major industries include renewable energy technology, marine biotechnology, precision manufacturing, and sustainable agriculture. Aurelia exports advanced desalination equipment, tidal power generators, pharmaceuticals derived from marine algae, and high-efficiency ceramic batteries.

The largest company headquartered in Aurelia is Lumina Dynamics, founded in 1988 by engineer Cassandra Holt. Lumina Dynamics develops tidal energy systems and employs more than 42,000 people worldwide. The company's flagship facility is located in Harbor Point Technology Park outside Solhaven.

Another major employer is BlueWave BioSystems, established in 1996. BlueWave specializes in pharmaceutical compounds extracted from deep-sea microorganisms and operates three offshore research laboratories.

The highest mountain in Aurelia is Mount Caelum, reaching an elevation of 4,082 meters above sea level. The mountain is located within the Emerald Highlands National Preserve and attracts thousands of hikers each year.

The country's longest river is the River Lys, flowing 684 kilometers from Mount Caelum to the Eastern Sapphire Sea. The River Lys supports extensive agriculture throughout the central plains and supplies drinking water to nearly 40 percent of the population.

Aurelia experiences a temperate maritime climate. Average summer temperatures range between 22 and 28 degrees Celsius, while winter temperatures generally remain between 8 and 15 degrees Celsius. Annual rainfall averages approximately 1,250 millimeters.

Education is compulsory between the ages of six and seventeen. Aurelia's literacy rate is reported at 99.4 percent. The nation's highest-ranked university is the University of Solhaven, founded in 1428 A.E., with internationally recognized engineering and ocean sciences programs.

The healthcare system is publicly funded through the National Health Authority. Citizens receive universal healthcare funded primarily through progressive income taxation. Average life expectancy is estimated at 84.6 years.

Transportation throughout Aurelia relies heavily on electrified high-speed rail. The Aurora Express connects Solhaven with the northern city of Northreach in just under two hours despite covering nearly 470 kilometers. Public transportation within major cities is free for residents under eighteen and over sixty-five years of age.

Aurelia generates approximately 93 percent of its electricity from renewable energy sources. Forty-five percent originates from offshore wind farms, thirty-one percent from tidal generators, twelve percent from geothermal plants, and five percent from hydroelectric facilities. The remaining electricity is supplied by natural gas reserve plants during periods of unusually high demand.

Tourism contributes roughly 11 percent of Aurelia's gross domestic product. Popular attractions include the Crystal Caverns, the Observatory of Tides, Emerald Highlands National Preserve, Harbor Point Lighthouse, and the annual Festival of Lights held every October in Solhaven.

The national animal of Aurelia is the Silver Crest Falcon, a bird found nowhere else in the world. The national flower is the Azure Bloom Orchid, which grows primarily in the mist forests surrounding Mount Caelum.

Aurelia celebrates Independence Day every year on June 18, commemorating its peaceful separation from the former Kingdom of Vesper in 1842. During the celebration, thousands of lanterns are released into the harbor at sunset.

The Ministry of Science operates the Aurora Deep Ocean Research Center, opened in 2018. Scientists there investigate deep-sea ecosystems, autonomous underwater robotics, and climate monitoring technologies. The center collaborates with universities from over twenty countries.

One of Aurelia's most ambitious engineering projects is the Horizon Sea Wall, completed in 2021 after eight years of construction. Stretching 62 kilometers along vulnerable coastal regions, the structure protects more than two million residents from storm surges and rising sea levels.

Food culture in Aurelia emphasizes seafood, citrus fruits, herbs, and volcanic-grown vegetables. Traditional dishes include Sunfish Stew, Citrus Rice Cakes, Ember Bread, and Ocean Herb Soup. The country's signature dessert is Moonstone Tart, made with vanilla cream and crystallized citrus peel.

Sports are an important part of Aurelian culture. Football is the most popular spectator sport, while sailing, rowing, and rock climbing are widely practiced recreationally. The National Stadium in Solhaven seats 82,000 spectators and hosted the 2032 Azure Games.

Environmental conservation is a major national priority. Approximately 38 percent of Aurelia's land area is designated as protected parks, forests, or marine reserves. The government has pledged to achieve net-zero carbon emissions by 2045 through continued investment in renewable energy and carbon capture technologies.
